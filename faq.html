Questions & answers

Answers to the questions families ask most. Tap a question to open it.

About the app

What is KeesApp?

The library app of Keerthana Music School. It holds the pieces you are learning, with their notation and recordings, lets you add your own notes and tags, find any piece quickly, and plan a concert programme with the shruthi for each item.

Who can use it?

Students and families of the school. Register with the school's form using your Google account; once the team approves you, you get a welcome email with the steps to install and sign in.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free, has no advertising, and is run by volunteers from the school community.

What do I need?

A Google account (the one you registered with) and any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser. Nothing is downloaded from an app store: you open the web page and add it to your home screen, and from then on it opens like an app. On iPhone and iPad, Safari is the simplest: its Share button has Add to Home Screen. Chrome can do it too on recent iPhones.

Your archive and the master archive

What is the master archive?

The school's own library of every piece taught at the school, looked after by the teachers and the IT team. You can browse the names of its pieces and ask for the ones you are learning with Request from Master Archive under Resources. Requests are reviewed each week.

What is my archive?

Your own library: a folder called Keerthana Archive in your own Google Drive. It holds the pieces you have received or added, with your recordings, notes and tags. Only you can see it.

How do pieces get into my archive?
  • Your teacher shares a piece (usually as a link in your class WhatsApp group): tap the link and it is added.
  • You request pieces from the master archive; once approved, the app offers to add them next time you open it.
  • You add your own notation PDFs or recordings with Add or Import.
When a piece is shared with me, do I get my own copy?

Yes. The notation and recordings are copied into your Keerthana Archive, so they stay yours even if the sender later deletes theirs, and you can find them in Google Drive without the app. The first time, Google shows you the files being copied: tick them and press Select. If you skip that step, the piece is still added, but as links to the sender's files.

Can I share a piece with a classmate?

Yes: tap Share on the piece and send the link. The notation, and any recordings you tick, become viewable by anyone with the link, so your classmate can open them and save their own copy. Recordings you don't tick stay private.

What happens if I remove a piece?

Nothing is deleted outright. Removing a piece moves it to a folder called Keerthana — marked for deletion in your Drive. You can move it back, or delete it there for good.

Where your files live, and security

Where are my files kept?

In your own Google Drive, in the Keerthana Archive folder. The app has no server or database of its own; it talks straight to your Drive from your phone.

Can the school or anyone else see my archive?

No. Your archive is private to your Google account. The only files others can open are ones you choose to share.

Why does Google ask me to allow Drive access?

So the app can save your pieces in your Drive. The permission it asks for only lets it see files it created itself or that you picked for it; the rest of your Drive stays invisible to it. You can withdraw it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What does the school keep about me?

Only your registration (name and Google email) and your piece requests, in a Google Sheet only the school's admins can open. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Will it use up my Google storage?

A little. Notation PDFs are small; recordings are bigger. Everything counts towards your Google account's free 15 GB.

How do I stop using it and remove everything?

Delete the Keerthana Archive folder from your Drive, remove KeesApp at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and email us to be taken off the members list. See Privacy Policy for the details.

Devices and updates

I have a new phone. What do I do?

Open the app's link on the new phone, add it to the home screen, and sign in with the same Google account. Everything is there: your archive, your starred pieces and your concert programmes, because they are kept in your Google Drive, not on the phone.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes: phone, tablet and computer at the same time. Sign in with the same Google account on each.

Do I need to reinstall when the app is updated?

No. The app updates itself: the next time you open it with an internet connection, you get the latest version. If something looks out of date, close the app fully and open it again.

Does it work offline?

The app opens without a connection, but your archive, notation and recordings are fetched from Google Drive, so you need internet to use them.

It says I am not a member, or shows an empty archive.

Check you signed in with the Google account you registered with: sign out and sign in again with that one. If you registered recently, the team approves new members each week; you will get a welcome email when you are in.

Help

Who do I ask for help?

Email KeesApp Help at keesapphelp@gmail.com. A volunteer from the school's IT team will get back to you.
