The Keerthana App

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 August 2026

The Keerthana App is a free web app made for the students, families and teachers of Keerthana Music School. It holds the school's repertoire of songs, the sheet music and notation that go with them, and lets you keep a personal library and plan concert programmes.

This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app has no server of its own and no database. Nothing you do in it is sent to us. Your personal library lives in your own browser and, if you sign in, in a single file in your own Google Drive.

Who runs the app

Keerthana Music School, Melbourne, Australia. Questions about this policy or about your data: prabhakar.ranganathan@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect nothing. The app is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no backend, no database, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. We cannot see what you search for, what you star, or which songs you open.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, the app asks Google for the following, and uses each only as described:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile) — to show you which account you are signed in as, and to decide whether you are a student, an admin or a teacher. The check happens in your browser against a list of email addresses for enrolled users built into the app. Your email is not transmitted to us or to anyone else every time you login i.e. we do not track use.
  • Per-file access to your Google Drive (drive.file) — so the app can save your personal library. It creates one file, keerthana-library.json, in your Google Drive, holding your starred songs, your concert programmes and any material you have added yourself, so that these follow you between your phone and your computer. This permission lets the app see only files it created itself or that you explicitly picked; the rest of your Google Drive stays invisible to it.
  • Full Google Drive access (drive) — requested only from teachers and admins, and only at the moment they first curate the shared archive. The school's music sheets are stored in a shared Drive folder that the app did not create, so per-file access cannot reach it. It is used solely to read and organise that archive.

The access token Google issues is held in memory for the duration of your visit and is discarded when you sign out or close the tab. We never see it.

Limited use of Google user data

The Keerthana App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: data obtained through these APIs is used only to provide and improve the features described above; it is never sold, never transferred to third parties, never used for advertising, and never read by humans except where you have asked us to help with a problem, where the law requires it, or for security purposes.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • your starred songs, concert programmes and any songs you have added yourself;
  • the identifiers of Drive folders you have pointed the app at, so it need not ask again.

A service worker also caches the app itself so that it opens without a connection. You can clear all of this at any time through your browser's "clear site data" setting.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose your information, because we do not hold it. Two other parties are unavoidably involved: Google, when you sign in and when the app reads or writes your Drive file, under Google's own privacy policy; and GitHub, which serves the app's files and, like any web host, sees the requests your browser makes.

Children

The app is used by school-age students. It collects no information about them beyond what is described above, and shows no advertising. Parents who would like their child's Drive file removed, or who have any concern, are welcome to write to the address above.

Removing your data

  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your library by removing keerthana-library.json from your Google Drive.
  • Clear the local copy using your browser's clear-site-data setting.

Because nothing is held on our side, these three steps remove everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
